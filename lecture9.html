Why does initialization matter?

What is wrong with zero initialization?

Identical neurons get identical updates, so they stay identical. Random weights break the symmetry, but their variance matters.

Forward variance in one linear layer

Assumes independent, zero-mean inputs and weights. Real networks satisfy this only approximately.

What problem does dropout address?

A deep network can fit its training data very well, yet generalize poorly.

Randomly disable hidden activations during training. At inference, use the full network.

Dropout: why we need inverted scaling

With inverted scaling, train and test agree, so no rescaling is needed at inference.

Why “approximate model averaging”?

  • Every mask defines a different subnetwork; all of them share their weights.
  • The full network approximates combining their predictions, but is not exactly an average of independently trained models.

Dropout in PyTorch

net = nn.Sequential(
    nn.Linear(100, 64),
    nn.ReLU(),
    nn.Dropout(p=0.5),
    nn.Linear(64, 1),
)

net.train()   # dropout is active
net.eval()    # dropout is disabled

AdamW (decoupled)

In Adam + L2, the decay term λθ passes through and , so weights with large gradients are decayed less. In AdamW every weight shrinks by the same factor (1 − ηλ).

Clipping handles large gradients. It does not restore gradients that have already vanished.
